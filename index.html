Welcome! My name is Carmen Yuchen Guo.

I am a graduate student in Computational Social Science at UC San Diego.

I work at the intersection of computational social science, data analytics, and machine learning , applying quantitative and qualitative methods to understand social and political phenomena, and to drive data-driven decision making.

Previously worked in a public affairs consultancy and higher education management, I bring a practical, human-centered perspective to data science, focusing on translating data into actionable insights. My broader interests include digital platform, public opinion analysis and AI for good.

I am currently pursuing my masters degree in Computational Social Science at University of California, San Diego. Before that, I obtained my M.S. in Marketing from The University of Hong Kong, and my B.A. in Advertising from Peking University.

Beyond research and professional work, I'm passionate about tennis, cinema, and photography.

Projects

Here are some projects I've worked on. Would love to hear any feedback and suggestions!

Causal Inference - Exposure to AI Overviews and News Consumption

A research design examining how Google's search engine AI Overviews (launched May 2024) affect user news consumption, using difference-in-differences framework.

Built a synthetic data generator producing search data and browsing records. Implemented a causal pipeline with first-stage exposure validation, parallel trends testing, and synthetic DiD robustness checks.

Poster

Text As Data - Variation in Party Influence in University Reports

Measures Chinese Communist Party ideological presence in university disclosure reports (2022–2024) using computational text analysis.

Manually annotated training data, trained three supervised classifiers (Naive Bayes, LASSO, random forest) to detect party-aligned content, then modeled whether variation is related to geographic location and institutional hierarchy.

GitHub

Character-Level RNN - LSTM model for Sherlock Holmes Text Generator

A PyTorch implementation of character-level LSTM text generation, trained on the complete Sherlock Holmes corpus.

Compared optimizers (RMSprop, SGD with momentum, Adam) and tuned hyperparameters including batch size, sequence length, dropout, and learning rate schedule.

GitHub

Deep Learning for Multilevel Regression and Poststratification

Deep learning extension of multilevel regression and poststratification (MRP) to estimate local-level climate opinion and policy attitudes from national survey data.
